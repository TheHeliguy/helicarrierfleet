S-61N POWER ASSURANCE Fig 2-2

⚠ When the check is required

For vertical take-off or landing operations, edge procedures, and operation from salt water, a power assurance check should be performed on each engine prior to each take-off.

How it is done

Operate the engine at 100% Nf on the ground or in a hover in ground effect, at the torque value specified on the Power Assurance Check Chart (Figure 2-2) in the cockpit, and record Ng, T5, OAT and altitude.

Success criteria

If the recorded values of Ng and T5 are less than those shown on the Pilot's Power Assurance Check Chart, then 2½ minute power is assured.

Indicator note: T5 values on the cockpit chart must be reduced 5°C when the Lewis pointer type indicator (P/N 71700 series) is installed.

Torque specified on your cockpit chart (Fig 2-2): 50–100 %
T5 limits used exactly as printed on the chart (Howell digital indicator).
ANTI-ICING: OFF
ICE SHIELD: OFF
Anti-ice and ice-shield condition are recorded with the check (Journey Log requirement) - no separate correction chart was supplied, so they do not change the calculated targets below.
